# Persona Addition Template

Use this template when adding new personas to docs/octoacme-roles-and-personas.md.

- Name:
- Role summary (1-2 lines):
- Responsibilities (bullet list):
- Key deliverables or artifacts:
- Interaction points (who they hand off to / who informs them):
- Primary escalation owner (who to escalate to for unresolved blockers):
- Example scenarios (optional):
- Acceptance criteria for adding the persona:
  - [ ] Clear responsibilities and handoffs documented
  - [ ] At least one owner named for escalation
  - [ ] Reviewed by PM and Product Lead
  - [ ] Added to Interaction Matrix (docs/octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)

Purpose: Standardize how new personas are proposed and added so that future additions are consistent and include necessary handoff details.
